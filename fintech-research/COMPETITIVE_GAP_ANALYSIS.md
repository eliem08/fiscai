**What we do NOT know:** whether accounting firms already have adequate free templates. Whether DGI's own portal computes enough. Odoo localization status. **These are the first interview questions.**

## B. Moroccan e-invoicing

Edicom, Comarch, Sage, Upsilon and Inyad all publish 2026 guides and products (S22–S24). Global CTC vendors will follow once the decree is published. **Gap only in timing and local support. Not defensible alone.**

## C. Customs pre-check (FiscAI PreClear)

No direct Moroccan product found in this pass **[H: not exhaustively searched]**. Indirect competition: customs brokers' expertise, ERP trade modules, and the PortNet platform itself. Absence of competitors ≠ opportunity.

## Conclusion

The only gap with **official evidence of pain + money already being spent + weak, single-vendor competition** is Moroccan payment-terms compliance. It becomes defensible only if it grows into **supplier finance**, because the declaration itself can be copied by any ERP.
