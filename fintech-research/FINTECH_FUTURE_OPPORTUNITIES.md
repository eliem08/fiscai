**Key insight:** a centralized, real-time-cleared e-invoicing regime (if Morocco implements CTC) turns every B2B invoice into a **government-verified receivable**. That makes SME receivables finance cheaper and safer. A company that already sits on companies' payables and receivables data, through 69-21 compliance, is positioned to finance them. **[H — depends on the final DGI model, which is not yet published.]**
