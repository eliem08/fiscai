## Conformance Verification Notes

1. **Independent Two-Tier Tenant Isolation (K.1):**
   - Application-layer scoping verifies tenant and client membership before loading entity state, returning 404 on missing or foreign IDs.
   - Forced PostgreSQL RLS applies on all 31 tenant tables via `USING (tenant_id = app.current_tenant())` and `WITH CHECK (tenant_id = app.current_tenant())`. Verified by `scripts/rls_check.py`.

2. **Jurisdiction Authority & Zero Domain Inventions (K.2, K.3):**
   - All accounting accounts, tax rates, withholding schedules, and issue codes originate in `/jurisdiction/ma/*.yaml`.
   - `scripts/check_no_tax_literals.py` continuously scans the codebase to ensure zero tax or legal numeric literals appear in application logic.
   - `scripts/generate_expert_sign_off.py --check` ensures every `needs_expert` entry is tracked in `docs/pilot/expert-sign-off-sheet.md`.

3. **Accounting Balancing & Rounding Mechanics (K.6, K.14):**
   - Property tests using Hypothesis confirm that every generated proposal balances debits and credits to the exact centime across single and multi-rate configurations.
   - Residual centimes resulting from foreign currency conversion are directed to explicit rounding lines (`rounding_loss` / `rounding_gain` accounts), leaving the counterparty balance strictly equal to invoice TTC converted at the transaction rate.

4. **Reconciliation Data Integrity & BQ Lifecycles (K.22, K.23):**
   - Database trigger `trg_sync_reconciliation_match_status` maintains status consistency between `reconciliation_matches` and `reconciliation_match_items`.
   - Partial unique index `idx_uq_accepted_bank_transaction` guarantees that no bank line can belong to two accepted matches simultaneously while allowing multiple proposed candidates.
   - Partial unique index `idx_uq_active_bq_proposal` guarantees that exactly one active BQ proposal exists per bank line across suspense, match acceptance, and match rejection lifecycles.

5. **Approval Security & Immutability (K.13, K.24):**
   - Approvals are restricted to monthly files. Professional qualification numbers are verified for accountants (`expert_comptable` or `comptable_agree`).
   - Re-authentication with multi-factor authentication (MFA) within 5 minutes is enforced per RFC 9470.
   - The canonical SHA-256 subject hash covers all active journal proposals and accepted matches, excluding volatile fields (timestamps, actor IDs, draft statuses). Any post-approval state mutation invalidates the hash and halts export generation.
