## Expansion logic (honest)

A Moroccan compliance product does **not** automatically scale to Africa: laws differ by country. Plausible paths:

1. **Francophone markets with similar instruments.** France's own payment-terms observatory and e-invoicing mandate show the pattern, but France is crowded. Tunisia, Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire are **[H: payment-terms and e-invoicing regimes not researched]**.
2. **Morocco as a hub for Moroccan banks in Africa.** Attijariwafa, BOA and BCP operate across West Africa **[H: channel, not validated]**.
3. **Product generalization.** Build "regulatory payables/receivables engine + finance" with country rule packs. This is defensible only after proving it in Morocco.

Venture-scale requires (2) or (3), or making the finance layer large in Morocco alone. **Morocco alone is probably a strong small business, not a venture-scale one, unless the finance layer works.** This is the core YC risk.
